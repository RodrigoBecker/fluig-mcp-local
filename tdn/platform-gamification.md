# Platform ❙ Gamification

# Index 

## Speaking of gamification ...

Gamification aims to engage users of the platform in a variety of activities, allowing for monitoring the results obtained and the progress achieved.

Through engagement actions users can earn titles, trophies, achievements, acquire experience in certain areas of knowledge, and strengthen their profiles on the platform.

Engagement activities can be as diverse as, for example, accessing the platform, posting contents, supporting posted content, joining communities, among others. Those activities score points that contribute to a user's advancement.

For this option to be displayed in the Applications container in the Control Panel, acquire the Gamification component from TOTVS Store.

### View related information to gamification

---

**01. **In the main menu, click **Control Panel**, then check **Application container** and click **Gamification**.

**02.** View the information related to gamification in its respective tabs.

### Add level

---

**01.** Click the tab **Levels**.

As you click that tab, you can view the window in which you can manage the levels of experience that can be acquired by users. Detailed information can be obtained from [Levels](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236430130).
