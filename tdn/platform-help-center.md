# Platform ❙ Help center

# Speaking of help...

---

The help center is intended for end users, administrators and developers. It contains links for documentation related to the content displayed on the page, and also allows users to search for specific topics. The links are classified as follows: User Manual, Technical Documentation, Frequently Asked Questions and Academy.

- [User guide](/display/fluigeng/Platform+%7C+User+guide) contains an overview of each feature and the steps required to perform the actions available in each one of them, as well as explanations about the data requested and relevant information on how to use the features.

- The [Technical documentation](/display/fluigeng/Platform+%7C+Technical+documentation) provides technical and advanced documentation related to the platform’s server management, settings and development.

- The [Frequently Asked Questions](/display/fluig/Plataforma+%7C+Perguntas+frequentes) gather answers to the most common questions asked by customers to the support team.

- The [TOTVS Fluig Academy](https://academy.fluig.com/) portal offers training on the platform.

The content displayed in help center takes into account the language chosen by the user for the platform, giving preference to documentation available in the same language. If the document is not available in the language chosen, the Portuguese version is suggested.

## Acess the help center 

---

**01.** Click on **User Menu** ![](/download/attachments/244422903/menu-usu%C3%A1rio.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747212160&api=v2) – located at the top right of the header.

If you have already changed your profile picture, it will be displayed in the **User Menu**, as follows: ![](/download/attachments/244422903/menu-usu%C3%A1rio-foto.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747302023&api=v2).

The location of the **User Menu** varies according to the [Theme](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236430025) selected by the administrator:

- In the **Classic** theme, the menu is located at the top right of the page next to the user's profile name and is identified by the **Settings** icon ![](/download/attachments/244422903/fluigicon-cog.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747302103&api=v2);

- In the **Snowflake** theme, it is positioned at the top left next to the user's profile picture and is accessed by the **More options** icon![](/download/attachments/244422903/flaticon-more-vert.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747302187&api=v2).

**02.** Click on ![](/download/attachments/244422903/%C3%ADcone-ajuda.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763746852793&api=v2) **Help**.

The documentation presented in the sidebar will be related to the feature being accessed at the moment the help is triggered.

Dica!

The Help Center can also be accessed by clicking on the **Help** icon****![](/download/attachments/244422903/question-off.png?version=1&modificationDate=1587997511263&api=v2), located at the top right of the header.

## Search documentation

---

**01.** Click on **User Menu** ![](/download/attachments/244422903/menu-usu%C3%A1rio.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747212160&api=v2) – located at the top right of the header.

If you have already changed your profile picture, it will be displayed in the **User Menu**, as follows: ![](/download/attachments/244422903/menu-usu%C3%A1rio-foto.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747302023&api=v2).

The location of the **User Menu** varies according to the [Theme](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236430025) selected by the administrator:

- In the **Classic** theme, the menu is located at the top right of the page next to the user's profile name and is identified by the **Settings** icon ![](/download/attachments/244422903/fluigicon-cog.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747302103&api=v2);

- In the **Snowflake** theme, it is positioned at the top left next to the user's profile picture and is accessed by the **More options** icon![](/download/attachments/244422903/flaticon-more-vert.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763747302187&api=v2).

**02.** Click on ![](/download/attachments/244422903/%C3%ADcone-ajuda.png?version=1&modificationDate=1763746852793&api=v2) **Help**.

**01.** Enter the term you wish to search for in the **Search** field.

**03.** The search starts automatically seconds after the terms are entered in the search field.

Users can also search for specific terms by clicking the magnifying glass icon next to the **Search** field.

**04.**The search results are listed below the search box, classified by the documentation type they represent.

The documentation types shown in help center are User Manual ([User guide](/display/fluigeng/Platform+%7C+User+guide)) and Technical Documentation ([Developer Portal](/display/fluigeng/Platform+%7C+Technical+documentation)).

## Display more search results

---

**01.** Enter the term you wish to search for in the **Search** field.

**02.** Click the****See more** link displayed below the category results.

The **See more** link is only shown for categories that return more than six results for the term searched. When clicking the link, users are directed to the portal for the category chosen: User Manual ([Fluig Help](https://tdn.totvs.com/display/HFE/Help+fluig+in+English)), Technical Documentation ([Technical documentation](/display/fluigeng/Platform+%7C+Technical+documentation)), FAQs ([Frequently questions](/display/fluigeng/Platform+%7C+Frequently+questions+-+TOTVS+Fluig+Platform)) and How-to *Videos* ([Fluig Learning](/display/AF)), where they can find all the content related to the search.

Please note!

This documentation is valid from the **Voyager (2.0)** update onwards. If you are using a previous update, it may contain information different from what you see on your platform.
