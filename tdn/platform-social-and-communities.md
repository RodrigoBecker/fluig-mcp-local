# Platform ❙ Social and Communities

# Index

## Speaking of Social and Communities...

The Social item allows accessing the latest news and comments posted by the user or their social network friends, such as followers or community members. As for the Communities item, it allows users to view all the communities in which they participate, in addition to adding communities they would like to join.

Social and Communities areas consist of features such as:

- [Posts](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429546): it allows users to create posts on their own timeline or in communities. In communities, in addition to timeline posts, users can post documents, pictures, images, videos, forms, and articles;

- [Actions in posts](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429582): it allows supporting, commenting, mentioning other users, reporting, sharing, following and deleting posts from the timeline and communities;

- [Relationships](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429645): this feature allows you to follow and be followed by the other users on the platform so you can view their profiles and follow their posts;

- [Participation in communities](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429660): it allows access to the content shared in the community such as posts, images, videos, forms, documents and articles;

- [Following communities](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429665): you can follow existing communities and receive notifications whenever a new post is added to the communities that you are following.
