# Platform ❙ Learning

In the learning environment, it is possible to have access to all learning activities that you attend or manage. It is possible to track information about classes, training, content, pending activities, notices, among others.

The learning environment consists of the following tabs:

- [My learning](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429677): this tab displays user active enrollments in training/tracks or in classes/subjects, the history of the courses that they attended and their extracurricular activities;

- [Learning activities](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429532): displays all of the authenticated user’s learning tasks, classified by date of completion and with the actions that can be executed in each type of task, such as answering an evaluation, accessing a track or a training course, accessing an event’s virtual room, among others;

- [Subjects and Classes](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429800): it allows for viewing the catalog of available subjects/classes, enabling users that have such permission to both manage and enroll in the chosen courses.

- [Tracks and Training](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429786): it allows for viewing the catalog of available tracks and training courses, enabling users that have such permission to both manage and enroll in them;

- [Manage](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429833): it allows for managing the items related to the contents used in the learning activities and of the skills that students can acquire by completing them;

- [Notices](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429844): this tab allows for managing the notices to be displayed to users, which may be specific to certain classes/subjects.

- [Events](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429840): here users can manage the already created events and also accept or decline the invitations that they have received to other users’ events;

- [Diary](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429842): here it is possible to view pending activities such as the execution of track/training or class, correction of activities, scheduling of evaluations, collaborative event sessions, among others;
- [Enrollments](/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=236429846): it allows for managing all the activities related to the enrollment in training/tracks and classes/subjects.
